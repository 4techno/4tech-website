# 4TECH project photography

Every photograph in `public/assets/projects/` is **representative imagery, not a photograph of the corresponding 4TECH build**. The portfolio and case studies display that distinction beside the image. They are contextual photographs of related engineering subjects, not evidence of project completion, validation, partnerships or endorsement by the pictured organizations. The project evidence records remain separate.

The images below were downloaded from their cited source pages on 4 October 2026 and converted to size-limited WebP files. Conversion changed encoding and image dimensions; no generated or composited content was added. Source pages and license terms should remain available with this project. Pinterest was used only as visual-direction research; no Pinterest image was copied because Pinterest search results alone do not establish reuse rights.
